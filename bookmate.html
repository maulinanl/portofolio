BOOKMATE

Android e-book management app to organize your personal library in one place.

Challenge

The Problem

Digital books end up scattered across folders and apps. Readers lose track of what they own, what they are reading, and where they stopped.

Goal: one simple place to manage an entire e-book collection.

Solution

The Approach

BookMate stores each book as a record in Firebase and gives the user a clean Flutter interface to add, browse, edit, and remove books, with the data always in sync.

Outcome: a focused library manager built around solid CRUD.

CRUD at the Core

01

Create

Add a new book with its details to the library.

02

Read

Browse the collection and open a book's detail page.

03

Update

Edit book information whenever something changes.

04

Delete

Remove books that are no longer needed.

Data Flow

01 / UIFlutter ScreenUser action such as adding or editing a book.
02 / LogicApp LogicInput is validated and prepared for saving.
03 / CloudFirebaseData is written to and read from the cloud.
04 / ResultUpdated UIThe interface refreshes with the latest data.
