Challenge

The Problem

Notes are usually stuck on one device or buried in chat threads, which makes sharing knowledge with classmates or teammates slow and messy.

Goal: make writing and sharing a note as easy as sending a message.

Solution

The Approach

NoteShare keeps the idea small: a clear interface for writing notes, a simple data structure in Firebase, and a sharing flow that lets others read them right away.

Outcome: a compact product that ties UI, structure, and data together.

Product Flow

01 / WriteCreate NoteUser writes a note in the editor.
02 / SaveStore in FirebaseThe note is saved to the cloud database.
03 / ShareShare NoteThe note is made available to other users.
04 / ReadView SharedOthers open and read the shared note.
